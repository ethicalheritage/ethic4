Privacy Policy

Legatum DeadDrop · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Legatum DeadDrop is designed to respect your privacy in the simplest possible way: we do not collect your data. The app runs entirely on your phone.

No servers of ours

The app does not collect, does not send and does not store personal data on the developer's servers. We have no server at all: there is nothing, anywhere, that we can see.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything you create — your messages, your recipients, your triggers and your settings — is stored on your device only, and it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM, with keys derived from your password). It is unlocked by your fingerprint or face plus your password, and it does not leave the phone except to send the messages you configured.

Sending your messages by email

To send your messages, you connect your own Google account once, using Google's official sign-in. The app asks for a single permission, "send email on your behalf" (the Gmail send scope), and uses it only to send the messages you configured, to the recipients you chose. The app does not read, import or store your existing emails. The access token stays encrypted on your device and is never sent to us.

Sending your messages by SMS

Your alerts can also be delivered as text messages, sent from your own phone number directly by the device — there is no third-party gateway and no server in between. Your mobile operator's standard SMS charges may apply. If you set up a check-in or command keyword, the app also looks at incoming text messages only to recognise that keyword; it does not read, store or transmit the content of your other messages.

Your location (optional)

If you choose to attach your location to an alert, the app includes it in the messages you configured, so your contacts know where to look for you. You can include either a fixed position you capture when preparing the message, or your live position at the moment the alert is sent. Your location is used only for this, is stored encrypted on your device like everything else, and is sent only to the recipients you chose. It is off unless you turn it on; the live option additionally needs the background-location permission.

Google API Services — Limited Use

Legatum DeadDrop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through the Gmail permission is used solely to provide the sending feature on your device and is never transferred to us or to any third party.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Fingerprint / face: to protect access to your data on the device.
  • Notifications: to show the background status and check-in reminders.
  • Running in the background: so the app can keep its timers reliably.
  • Send SMS: to deliver your alerts as text messages to the numbers you chose.
  • Receive SMS (optional): only if you set a check-in or command keyword, to recognise it in incoming messages.
  • Location (optional): only if you enable attaching your position to an alert; background location only for the live-position option.
  • Device administrator (optional): only if you enable a security action that needs it; you grant it explicitly and can revoke it in your device settings.

No tracking

No tracking, no statistical analysis (analytics), no advertising. The app does not make money from your data.

Your rights

You are in control of everything on your device. You can delete all of the app's data at any time from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Since we never hold your data, there is nothing of yours for us to delete on our side. If you are in the EU, you may also contact your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
