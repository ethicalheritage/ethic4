the alert that sends itself

What it does

You decide what to say and exactly when it goes out. Then you just check in. If the moment arrives and you haven't, the app delivers for you.

Delivered by SMS

A text message doesn't need internet, an app, or a server to arrive. It reaches your contacts even where there's no data signal — the most reliable channel when it matters.

…and by email

Sent through your own Google account, connected once. The app uses it only to send the messages you configured — it never reads or imports your inbox.

Everything stays on your phone

Messages and contacts are encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM, keys derived with Argon2id) and unlocked with your password — plus your fingerprint, if you turn it on. Nothing reaches us — there is no "us".

Remote check-in by SMS

Reset your timer, suspend or re-arm your alert, or fire it — with a single text message. Schedule arming for a set date and time, or after a delay. Keep the alert on a dedicated phone and drive it from afar, with no app to open in the moment. Remote commands stay off until you turn them on, and they work only from the numbers you authorise, with a dedicated password.

Your location, if you want

Optionally attach your position to the alert — fixed when you prepare the message, or live at the moment it is sent — so your contacts know where to look for you.

Triggers you control

A recurring check-in, a specific date and time, repeated failed access attempts, or an SMS keyword — combined with AND / OR logic.

One alert for everyone, or a personal message per contact. Each recipient can receive more than one copy, so a single failure doesn't silence the alert.

Who it's for

For anyone who may be out of reach when it counts.

Solo explorers & hikers

Arm an alert that fires if you don't check in within the time you set, so your contacts are warned automatically if something happens to you.

Lone workers in remote sites

Before a potentially dangerous task, set an alert that goes out if you're not back safe within the window you planned.

A dedicated, remote-driven device

Keep a timed alert on a spare phone, armed, suspended or triggered from afar by a simple text message — no signal needed where you're going.

How it works

Four steps, then it waits.

1 · Write your alert

Write the messages and add the contacts who should receive them.

2 · Choose when

A recurring check-in, a date and time, failed access attempts, or a combination.

3 · Check in

Tap check-in whenever you're OK — in the app, from the reminder notification, or by text message — to reset the timers.

4 · It delivers

If the conditions you set are met, your alert goes out automatically, by SMS and email.

Your data stays yours

No account on our side, no sign-up, no profile. Everything you create lives on your phone, encrypted. There is no server of ours to hold it.

Sending email through your account

The app asks Google for one permission only — "send email on your behalf" — and uses it solely to send the messages you configured. It never reads, imports or stores your other mail, and the access token stays encrypted on your device.

Sending text from your number

SMS alerts are sent from your own phone number, straight from the device. No third-party gateway, no server in between.

Important — please read. Legatum DeadDrop runs entirely on your device and depends on it. Delivery cannot be guaranteed if the phone is switched off, out of battery, restarted and not yet unlocked, out of signal, or stopped by the system from running in the background. It is a personal safety tool — not a guaranteed or life-critical alerting service, and not a substitute for emergency services.
