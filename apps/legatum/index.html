Legatum DeadDrop

encrypted messages, delivered when you can't

Prepare the messages that matter to the people who matter, and choose exactly when they should be sent. If the moment arrives, Legatum DeadDrop sends them for you, by email.

Android Free · no ads · no purchases 30 languages No account, no server

What it does

Scheduled, encrypted message delivery that runs entirely on your phone.

Your message

Write one message for everyone, or a personal message for each recipient. Everything is stored encrypted on your device and only you can unlock it, with your fingerprint or face plus your password.

Your triggers

Choose when it should go out: a recurring check-in (minutes, hours or days), a specific date and time, repeated failed access attempts — or a combination, with AND / OR logic.

Automatic delivery

Confirm you're OK with a single tap whenever you want to reset the timers. If the conditions you set are met, the app sends your message by email, on its own.

Please know. Legatum DeadDrop runs entirely on your device and depends on it. Delivery cannot be guaranteed if the phone is switched off, out of battery, offline, or prevented by the system from running. It is a personal tool, not a guaranteed or life-critical delivery service.

Built for privacy

Strong encryption

Your messages and recipients are stored encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM, with keys derived from your password using Argon2id.

Local multi-factor

Your fingerprint or face, plus a password, are required to read or change anything — and that check happens only on your phone.

Your own channel

Emails are sent through your own dedicated Google account, which you connect once. There is no account on our side.

Your data stays yours

No account, no sign-up, no profile. What you create stays on your phone: a server of ours does not exist. No tracking, no analytics, no ads.

Signing in with Google

The app asks for one permission only: to send email on your behalf. It uses it solely to send the messages you configured, and it does not read, import or store your existing emails. The developer has no access to your account.

Nothing leaves without you

Your data never leaves the device except to send the messages you set up, to the recipients you chose. You can delete everything at any time from within the app.

Worth knowing. For reliable sending we recommend a dedicated Google account with 2-Step Verification. You stay in control: you decide the message, the recipients and the conditions — the app only carries them out.
