Privacy Policy

TUTORME · Last updated: 4 September 2026

TUTORME is designed to respect your privacy in the simplest possible way: we do not collect your data.

No servers of ours

The app does not collect, does not send and does not store personal data on the developer’s servers. We have no server at all: there is nothing, anywhere, that we can see.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything about you — progress, lessons taken, settings — is stored on your device only, in a local database. It does not leave the phone, except in the cases explained below, which you decide.

What the app does NOT keep

The texts you send to Verify and your debate conversations are not saved anywhere: not on your device, not in the saved configuration, nowhere. They stay in memory only for as long as it takes to show you the answer, then they are gone when you leave the screen.

This is a deliberate choice: data that is not kept cannot be lost, read or handed to anyone. The less an app keeps about you, the less there is to protect.

Backup (optional, on YOUR Google Drive)

If you wish, you can back up your settings. The backup is then saved on your own Google Drive, in a folder called "TUTORME Backup", which remains under your exclusive control. The developer has no access to that folder or to your Drive.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for your permission solely in order to write the backup file to your Drive.

AI provider key — optional

To improve how the app works you can set up some recommended AI providers using an API key of your own, or you can set up a different provider by configuring it with your own parameters. The key and the settings are yours, they remain encrypted on your device (and, if applicable, in your backup on your own Google Drive) and they use your personal quota. The developer never sees this data, there is no shared key, and there is no server of ours in between.

When you use fact-checking or the lessons

To generate the lessons or check a claim, the text you send is processed by an artificial-intelligence provider and protected under that provider’s own privacy policy.

Fact-checking sources

To find corroboration, the app contacts public websites directly from your device (news agencies, fact-checkers, broadcasters, scientific sources). These are public sites, just as when you open a web page.

To find facts that a news feed might no longer show, the app also queries some public search services directly from your device. Only the words of your claim are sent, with no identifier, and each service applies its own policy.

No tracking

No tracking, no statistical analysis (analytics). The app is completely free and does not make money from your data.

Your rights

You can delete all of the app’s configuration data at any time: open Settings, then "Manage app data", and tap "Reset to initial settings". This resets the app to how it was when freshly installed. Since the app does not keep the texts you send or your debates, there is nothing else of that kind left to delete.
